Erdős 687(i) | Research handoff from francoisbrunel with Codex

The open target asks for the order of Y up to constant factors. The companion
sources concern finite covers, one-sided bounds and obstacles to some methods.

Proof sources

  Couvertures687.lean        Finite witnesses for 47, 71 and 73, and the
                            explicit double cover of the 22 admissible points.
  Exclusion687.lean          Soundness of exhaustive rejection using a sum of
                            prime capacities, with two bounded applications.
  Comptage687.lean           Explicit bound on pi(x) in the integer
                            normalization used by the progression estimate.
  ReductionIwaniec.lean      Replay name of the R2 reduction source
                            reductions/Erdos687.erdos_687.variants.iwaniec.R2.lean.
  Transfert687.lean          Bridges to Green46.IsCoveredByResidues and
                            Erdos687.Y, including Y(37) = 65, Y(47) >= 99,
                            Y(73) >= 189, parity, counting and analytic transfer.
  Progressions687.lean       Uses the Chinese remainder theorem to embed the
                            survivors of one progression in a sifted interval.

and lake-manifest.json. Run the following from the checkout root. Copy the six
Lean sources listed above to that root, using the replay names. The other files
are independent of the Lean build. Quoting below is for a POSIX shell.

Audit687.lean should be copied to the same directory before the final command.
The declarations are suitable for a #print axioms audit. The computational
proofs use the kernel's decide. The final assertions must be read with all
their hypotheses, even if the audit reports only standard axioms.

Scope and remaining inputs

  The proposed O(x² / (log x)^(401/200)) estimate is one-sided. Its general
  transfer takes a lower bound on survivors, an upper bound per progression
  and a bound on a positive prime harmonic sum. The TeX note explains the
  source theorems and constants. These analytic estimates need formal proofs
  in the same environment to make the bound unconditional in Lean.
  log_saving_at_cutoff specializes to exactly the stock and class scales in
  the note, proving positivity and the conversion of the floored block length.

  For the quadratic Iwaniec variant, H1 and H2 are hypotheses with the same
  absolute threshold, separating survivors at x/2 from the sum of the further
  deletions on [1,x²]. The old R1 split instead uses a shifted gap theorem and
  Mertens' product. The two splits are separate contributions.
  H2_from_progression discharges prime counting using normalized_prime_count.
  A uniform bound on the survivors in one large prime's progression still
  suffices for H2. H1 remains a separate sieve input.
  H2_from_index_sieve expresses that remaining upper estimate on an ordinary
  interval of floor(x^2/p)+1 consecutive indices with arbitrary residues.

  The universal exclusion of 184 positions with primes through 71 and one
  extra singleton is certified computationally by two independent Python
  searches. The Lean witnesses at 183 and 189 and the implication forcing
  distance 146 between two still-uncovered points are separate results.

  The restricted one-singleton model, the cyclic gap formula, block estimates,
  semiprime loss construction in a thin or fixed-power band, grouped-capacity
  obstruction and common-representative obstruction are documented in the
  report and TeX note. The Lean sources give the finite or conditional pieces
  explicitly named there; the prose arguments retain their own proof status.
